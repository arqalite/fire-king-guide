#### Steps
- Normal Summon [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_].
- Activate Ulcanix to destroy [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] in your hand and add [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Ponix to Special Summon itself from hand.
- Activate Ponix to add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sanctuary; place [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
- Activate Island to destroy Ulcanix and add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from Deck to hand.
- A chain forms:
    - Activate Ulcanix to Special Summon [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] from Deck.
    - Activate Sacred to Special Summon itself from hand.
- Activate Sacred to destroy [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] from Deck.
- Activate Kirin to Special Summon Ulcanix from GY.
- Link Summon [_Salamangreat Sunlight Wolf_] using Sacred and High Garunix.
- Link Summon [_Relinquished Anima_] using Ponix.
- Activate Sunlight Wolf to add Kirin from GY to hand.
- Link Summon [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_] using Anima and Wolf.
- Activate Princess to Special Summon Arvata from GY.
- Link Summon [_Amphibious Swarmship Amblowhale_] using Princess and Ulcanix.
