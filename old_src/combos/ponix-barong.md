# Ponix + Barong

Destroying Barong gives us the opportunity to grab a copy of [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] in the Standby Phase; this can insulate our plays against our opponent's shenanigans, by ensuring we have ways to destroy our monsters and getting the interruption sequence started.

This means that generally you want to avoid extending with [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] or [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] - using their effects to revive Barong will stop his effect from activating. Thus, [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_] is your best friend, allowing you to extend while keeping Barong in the GY.

He also enables a pretty nifty combo that is Nibiru-safe, let's see it in action.

#### Steps
- Normal Summon [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_].
- Activate Ponix - add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from Deck.
- Activate Sanctuary - place [_Fire King Island_] in Field Zone.
- Activate Island - destroy [_Fire King Avatar Barong_] in hand, add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from Deck.
- Activate Sacred in hand - Special Summon it to the field.
- Activate Sacred on field - destroy [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_] from Deck.
- Activate Ulcanix in GY - Special Summon [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] from Deck.
- Link Summon [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_].

#### Opponent's turn
**Standby Phase:**
- Activate Barong to add [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] from Deck to hand.

**When you're ready to interrupt:**
- Activate Kirin to destroy Princess.
- Activate Sacred to Special Summon itself from the GY.
- Activate Sacred to destroy [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] from Deck.
- Activate Arvata to revive High Avatar Garunix from GY.
- When ready, Xyz Summon [_Garunix Eternity, Hyang of the Fire Kings_] using Sacred and High Garunix.
- Activate Hyang to blow up the board, *including Kirin*.
- Activate Kirin to revive Arvata from GY.

**Further interruption:**
- When a monster effect is activated, activate Arvata to negate the activation, then destroy Hyang.
- Activate Hyang to revive Ulcanix and Ponix.
- A chain forms:
    - Ulcanix effect to destroy Ponix and add [_Fire King Avatar Rangbali_];
    - Ponix effect to add any Fire King Spell you might need.
- Activate Rangbali to Special Summon it (as Ponix was destroyed).

> At this point, you still have a Rangbali negate (destroying Ulcanix), and an unused Princess in GY (destroying a used Rangbali, or Ulcanix if Rangbali hasn't come up yet).
