### Duelittle Chimera setup (Nibiru-safe, 1 Fuwalos draw)

This route tries to play lower to the ground by setting up the Fire King engine, destroying a [_Fire King Avatar Barong_] off [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_], and ending on a [_Duelittle Chimera_].

In the Standby Phase, Ponix returns to hand, and Barong will search a [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_], replenishing your hand and also giving you a way to start the interruption chain.

#### Steps
- Normal Summon [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_].
- Activate Ulcanix to destroy the Fire King in your hand and add [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Ponix to Special Summon itself from hand.
- Activate Ponix to add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sanctuary; place [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
- Activate Island to destroy Ponix and add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sacred to Special Summon itself.
- Activate Sacred to destroy [_Fire King Avatar Barong_] from Deck.
- Link Summon [_Duelittle Chimera_] using Sacred and Ulcanix.
- Pass turn.

##### Opponent's turn
- In Standby Phase:
    - Activate Barong to add [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] from Deck to hand.
    - Activate Ponix to add itself back to hand.
- When you're ready to interrupt, activate Kirin to destroy Duelittle Chimera and Special Summon itself.
- A chain forms:
  - Activate Sacred to Special Summon itself from GY.
  - Activate Chimera to add something back to hand.
- Activate Sacred to destroy anything you might need from Deck, to continue interrupting your opponent.
