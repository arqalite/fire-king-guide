### Amblowhale setup (with Ponix & Kirin in hand, via Sunlight Wolf)

Continuing on, this route still ends on an [_Amphibious Swarmship Amblowhale_], but instead of setting up [_Duelittle Chimera_], we choose to use the Sunlight Wolf recycling pattern to grab a [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_].

This gives us additional redundancy in case we are unable to blow up Amblowhale with [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_], since we can use Kirin to do that instead. Otherwise, alongside the Ponix that returns to your hand in the Standby Phase, you have a lot of follow-up for turn 3.

#### Steps
- Normal Summon [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_].
- Activate Ulcanix; destroy the Fire King in your hand and add [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] from Deck to hand, then make Ulcanix Level 1.
- Activate Ponix; Special Summon itself from hand.
- Activate Ponix; add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sanctuary; place [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
- Activate Island; destroy Ponix and add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sacred; Special Summon itself.
- Activate Sacred; destroy [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] from Deck.
- Activate Kirin; Special Summon a Fire King from GY, **except Ponix**.
- Link Summon [_Salamangreat Sunlight Wolf_] using Sacred and the revived Fire King.
- Link Summon [_Relinquished Anima_] using Ulcanix.
- Activate Wolf; add Kirin from GY to hand.
- Link Summon [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_].
- Activate Princess; Special Summon a FIRE monster from GY, **except Ponix**.
- Link Summon [_Amphibious Swarmship Amblowhale_].

##### Opponent's turn
- In Standby Phase, activate Ponix to add itself back to hand.
