### Amblowhale + Arvata setup (with Duelittle Chimera in GY)

This route chooses to end on an [_Amphibious Swarmship Amblowhale_] backed up by [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_]. Additionally, we set up [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_] and [_Duelittle Chimera_] in the GY to take full advantage of Amblowhale's effects.

This line chooses to destroy [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_] instead of [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_], giving up Ponix's recursion for additional Link material so we can keep Arvata on the field.

#### Steps
- Normal Summon [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_].
- Activate Ulcanix; destroy the Fire King in your hand and add [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Ponix; Special Summon itself from hand.
- Activate Ponix; add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sanctuary; place [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
- Activate Island; destroy Ulcanix and add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from Deck to hand.
- A chain forms:
    - Activate Sacred; Special Summon itself.
    - Activate Ulcanix; Special Summon [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] from Deck.
- Activate Sacred; destroy [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] from Deck.
- Activate Arvata; Special Summon any Fire King from GY.
- Link Summon [_Duelittle Chimera_].
- Link Summon [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_].
- Activate Princess; Special Summon Arvata from GY.
- Link Summon [_Amphibious Swarmship Amblowhale_] with Princess and the other monster besides Arvata.
