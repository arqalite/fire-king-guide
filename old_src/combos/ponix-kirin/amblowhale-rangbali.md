### Amblowhale + Rangbali setup (with Kirin in hand)

This route chooses to go for maximum value (and flashiness), ending on a [_Amphibious Swarmship Amblowhale_] backed up by [_Fire King Avatar Rangbali_], while also going through a Sunlight Wolf recycle to grab Kirin back from the GY.

#### Steps
- Normal Summon [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_].
- Activate Ponix - add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from Deck.
- Activate Sanctuary - place [_Fire King Island_] in Field Zone.
- Link Summon [_Salamangreat Almiraj_].
- Activate Island - destroy [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] in hand, add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from Deck.
- Chain:
  - CL1 Sacred in hand - Special Summon it to the field.
  - CL2 Kirin in GY - Special Summon Ponix from GY.
- Activate Sacred on field - destroy [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_] from Deck.
- Activate Ulcanix in GY - Special Summon [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] from Deck.
- Link Summon [_Salamangreat Sunlight Wolf_] using High Garunix and Almiraj.
- Link Summon [_Relinquished Anima_] using Ponix.
- Activate Sunlight Wolf - add High Avatar Kirin from GY to hand.
- Link Summon [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_] using Wolf and Anima.
- Activate Princess - Special Summon Ulcanix from GY.
- Activate Ulcanix - destroy Sacred, add [_Fire King Avatar Rangbali_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Rangbali - Special Summon it to the field.
- Link Summon [_Amphibious Swarmship Amblowhale_] using Princess and Ulcanix.
