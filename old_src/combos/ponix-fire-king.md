# Ponix + any Fire King

Adding a second card to the 1-card Ponix combos makes them so much more powerful, allowing you to add multiple layers of interruption alongside the board clear that [_Garunix Eternity, Hyang of the Fire Kings_] offers.

Depending on the second card, though, the combos start to vary slightly if you want to take full advantage of the second card. As such, we'll cover each specific 2-card combo in individual pages, discussing what makes it unique and what you should look out for.

That said, all those combos build on the same [play patterns] we covered earlier in this book, following most of the same steps and only diverging where necessary. Thus, we have access to a handful of generic combos that can be reused for any opening hand where you have [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] and a Fire King monster. 

> While inefficient, they can be a good starting point for learning the deck - learn these combos first before diving in and learning the individual combos for each 2-card opening hand.
