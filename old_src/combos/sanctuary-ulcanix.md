# Sanctuary + Ulcanix

As you might remember, [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_] needs another FIRE monster in order to use her effect and start a combo. However, when we already have Sanctuary in our opening hand, we no longer need Ulcanix's effect, so that's not an issue.

Instead, we can use her directly as Island fodder, and combo from there, enjoying protection from both [_Nibiru, the Primal Being_] and [_Droll & Lock Bird_].

#### Steps
- Activate [_Fire King Sanctuary_]; place [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
- Activate Island; destroy [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_] for [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_].
- A Chain forms:
    - Activate Sacred in hand; Special Summon it to the field.
    - Activate Ulcanix in GY; Special Summon [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] from Deck.
- Activate Sacred on field; destroy [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] from Deck.
- Activate Arvata; Special Summon Ulcanix from GY.
- Link Summon [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_].
- Activate Princess; Special Summon Arvata from GY.
