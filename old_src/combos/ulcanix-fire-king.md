# Ulcanix + any Fire King

As with the Ponix + any Fire King combos, these are generic combos that can be done with any Fire King in hand. The rest of the pages in the Ulcanix section will showcase the different nuances of each specific 2-card hand, but these are perfectly fine when starting out, or as a quick reference.

> In all the videos on this page, treat [_Fire King Avatar Rangbali_] as a generic Fire King with no effect.
