### Duelittle Chimera setup (with Barong in GY)

This route takes advantage of [_Fire King Avatar Barong_] in order to play as little as possible during our turn. 

By grabbing [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] with Barong, we can play during the opponent's turn, avoiding [_Nibiru, the Primal Being_], while still extracting value from the Ash Blossom via [_Duelittle Chimera_] and setting up a torrent of interruptions for your opponent to deal with.

#### Steps
**Your turn**:
- Normal Summon [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_].
- Ponix effect - add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sanctuary - place [_Fire King Island_] from Deck into the Field Zone.
- Activate Island - destroy [_Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring_] in hand; add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sacred - Special Summoning itself.
- Activate Sacred - destroying [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] from Deck.
- Link Summon [_Duelittle Chimera_] using Sacred and Ponix.
- Pass turn.

**Opponent's turn**:
- In Standby Phase, activate Barong to add [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] from Deck to hand.
- When you're ready to interrupt, activate Kirin to destroy Duelittle Chimera and Special Summon itself.
- A chain forms:
  - Activate Sacred to Special Summon itself from GY.
  - Activate Chimera to add Ash Blossom back to hand.
- Activate Sacred to destroy [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_] from Deck.
- Activate Ulcanix to Special Summon [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] from Deck.

**Further interruption:**
- When appropriate, Xyz Summon [_Garunix Eternity, Hyang of the Fire Kings_] using both Garunixes.
- Hyang effect to wipe the board, including Kirin.
- Kirin effect, Special Summon Ulcanix from GY (and pop a card if needed).
- Ulcanix effect - destroy Hyang, search [_Fire King Avatar Rangbali_].
- A Chain forms:
    - CL1: Hyang effect to Special Summon any varation of Barong, High Garunix and/or Ponix depending on what kind of follow-up you want
    - CL2: Rangbali effect to Special Summon itself.
