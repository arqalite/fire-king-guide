### Amblowhale + Arvata setup (with Kirin in hand)

This route allows us to end on [_Amphibious Swarmship Amblowhale_] + Arvata, while still getting Kirin back to hand with the Sunlight Wolf recycling pattern.

#### Steps
- Normal Summon [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_].
- Activate Ulcanix; destroy [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] and add [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] from Deck to hand, then make her Level 1.
- Activate Ponix; Special Summon it from hand.
- Activate Ponix; add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sanctuary; place [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
- Activate Island; destroy Ulcanix and add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from Deck to hand.
- A Chain forms:
  - Activate Sacred; Special Summon it.
  - Activate Ulcanix; Special Summon [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] from Deck.
- Activate Sacred; destroy [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] from Deck.
- Activate Arvata; Special Summon Ulcanix from GY.
- Link Summon [_Salamangreat Sunlight Wolf_] with High Garunix and Sacred.
- Link Summon [_Relinquished Anima_] using Ponix.
- Activate Wolf; add Kirin from GY to hand.
- Link Summon [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_].
- Activate Princess; Special Summon Arvata from GY.
- Link Summon [_Amphibious Swarmship Amblowhale_] with Princess and Ulcanix.
