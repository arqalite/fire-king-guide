### Princess + Arvata setup (with Ponix and Kirin in hand)

This route allows us to end on [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_] + Arvata, while still getting Kirin back to hand with the Sunlight Wolf recycling pattern.

Ponix also returns to your hand in the Standby Phase, making this line very hand-efficient.

#### Steps
- Normal Summon [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_].
- Activate Ulcanix; destroy [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] and add [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] from Deck to hand, then make her Level 1.
- Activate Ponix; Special Summon itself from hand.
- Activate Ponix; add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sanctuary; place [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
- Activate Island; destroy Ponix and add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sacred; Special Summon itself.
- Activate Sacred; destroy [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] from Deck.
- Activate Arvata; Special Summon Kirin from GY.
- Link Summon [_Salamangreat Sunlight Wolf_] with Sacred and Kirin.
- Link Summon [_Relinquished Anima_] using Ulcanix.
- Activate Wolf; add Kirin from GY to hand.
- Link Summon [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_].
- Activate Princess; Special Summon Arvata from GY.

##### Opponent's turn
- In Standby Phase, activate Ponix to add itself back to hand.
