### Princess + Arvata setup

This route trades access to [_Fire King Sanctuary_] for Nibiru safety - so you have 1 less interruption than you normally would have, but that's still miles better than ending on a Nibiru token.

#### Steps
- Activate [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
- Activate Island; destroy [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_] to search [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_].
- A Chain forms:
    - Activate Sacred; Special Summon it.
    - Activate Ulcanix; Special Summon [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_].
- Activate Sacred; destroy [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_].
- Activate Arvata; Special Summon Ulcanix from GY.
- Link Summon [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_].
- Activate Princess; Special Summon Arvata from GY.
