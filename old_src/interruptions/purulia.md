Welp, try not to cry as you Normal Summon [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] and your opponent immediately draws a card.

Yeah, this kinda cooks us. We need our Normal Summon, and we generally summon from hand at least twice during all of our combos (make that three if we start with [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_]. 

There's really no guaranteed way to avoid it. Thus, we recommend just powering through it and hoping your opponent draws the worst 2-3 cards in their entire deck.

> Similar to its other Mulcharmy siblings, you can also just Chain [_Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring_] to this card's activation, if you have it in your opening hand.
