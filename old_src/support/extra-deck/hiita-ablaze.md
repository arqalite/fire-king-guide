Hiita is a godsend when it comes to helping us recover from an [_Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring_], or extending further against other FIRE decks.

You can summon this, steal a FIRE monster, make [_Promethean Princess,
Bestower of Flames_], revive a monster, and make [_Amphibious Swarmship Amblowhale_] - [see the pattern here].

Later on she can help you Link-climb a little easier, if you're running other
Link-3 or higher monsters and you need to summon them.

And if she's ever destroyed (usually you Link her off immediately after using her effect so this doesn't come up that often), she can search any FIRE monster with 1500 DEF or less.

<b style="color: var(--sidebar-active);">We heavily recommend running 1 copy if you own it.</b>
