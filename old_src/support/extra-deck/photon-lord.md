Some opening hands are barely playable - you're able to put out 2 Level 8s on the field, but don't have [_Fire King Sanctuary_] available to Hyang on the opponent's turn.

Photon Lord _can_ come up during those situations, giving you something to end on (although [_S:P Little Knight_] can also fill that role).

That happens quite rarely, so experiment with him and see if it has a place in your games.
