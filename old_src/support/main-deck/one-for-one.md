Pitch a handtrap, grab Ponix - 'nuff said.

It's a minor, but helpful consistency boost, so throwing one copy in your deck never hurts if you have the space.

If you run this, we also recommend running [_Relinquished Anima_] in your Extra Deck - you cannot Link Summon [_Salamangreat Almiraj_] using a Special Summoned [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_], as Almiraj specifically requires a Normal Summoned monster. Anima can fulfill the same role as Almiraj in your combos, alongside
its monster-stealing effect, so it's worth having around.
