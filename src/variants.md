# Variants and Engines

So far, we talked about Fire King as a standalone deck, without any other engines or archetypes mixed in. You've seen how it functions, what it can do and both its strengths and its shortcomings. 

Some of you might be content with that - pure Fire King is a capable and fun deck on its own, but some players might be looking for a little bit more. Be it a secondary engine to compensate for some weaknesses, or a variant that plays in a unique way - players have tried and tested many of them, and it would be unfair to ignore their efforts.

Let's spend a brief moment talking about these variants and what makes them appealing.

> Keep in mind this section is very brief and only serves as an introduction to these variants. If you're looking for more information, check the dedicated channels on [the Fire King Discord server].
