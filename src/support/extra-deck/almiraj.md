Almiraj is a nifty card.

First of all, it makes the Ponix + any Fire King combos possible, as we can Link Summon this using Ponix in order to have a target for Arvata/Kirin.

Additionally, it enables us to perform the [_Sunlight Wolf recycling pattern_] more easily, by using just 1 material to summon something under Sunlight Wolf.

And last but not least, it can turn any handtrap into a FIRE monster, to make [_Fire King Island_] plays with, in case you didn't open a good hand.

Its effects aren't too relevant but in some situations you can protect a monster from being destroyed by card effects, so that never hurts to have.

<b style="color: var(--sidebar-active);">We heavily recommend running 1 copy if you own it.</b>
