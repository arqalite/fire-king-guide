# Introduction to Fire King Tri-Brigade

Welcome to the tri-brigade variant!

As the title implies, in this section we aim to explore the combination of a Fire King deck with the Tri-Brigade archetype for casual or competitive play.

> This section assumes you have read the majority of the book already or have previous experience with the Fire King archetype. 

We'll begin by learning what makes this variant different from a pure Fire King deck, the <span style = "color: green;">pros</span> and <span style = "color: red;">cons</span> of playing the deck and it's general strategy.

Then we'll focus on each tri-brigade card and discuss it's role in the deck and the ratios you might want to try while deckbuilding. We'll also look at some non-engine that this variant can play compared to pure.

Finally we'll look at different combos you should know, the ability of said combos to play through handtraps, what to do if your oponent interrupts your plays and some tips for maximizing your chances of winning on specific situations.
