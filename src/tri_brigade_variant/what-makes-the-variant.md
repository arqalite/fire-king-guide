# What makes a Fire King Tri-Brigade deck

Tri-Brigade is an archetype that revolves arround a "Tri-Type", this being **Beasts, Beast Warriors and Winged Beasts**. These are used as a way of getting link monsters into the field for powerful effects or disruptions. By managing our graveyard we are able to put up endboards with layered interruption while also generating card advantage with the different spells and trp cards the archetype provides.

When combining both archetypes we can boost the ceiling of the deck while also giving it some useful tools the pure variant can't access consistently. While in paper this combination looks great, it doesn't come without it's downsides, mostly in the name of consistency issues and difficulty when playing through handtraps.

At the time of writing there are 17 Tri-Brigade cards in the TCG (and 1 lore card). We'll explain each card in detail and provide ratios that maximize the consistency of the deck, but don't be afraid to play arround with them and find what suits best your style and needs.
