# Ulcanix + Ash Blossom

As with the [Ponix + Ash Blossom] combos, it's often in our best interest to maximize value from the [_Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring_] and avoid letting her sit in the GY. As such, we'll make use of the [Sunlight Wolf recycling pattern] or [_Duelittle Chimera_] to recover Ash and ensure we are still able to use her as an interruption during the opponent's turn.
