# Ulcanix + Sacred

This starting hand enables an interesting line of play - as [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] is already in the GY when you use [_Fire King Island_]'s effect, you can just opt to search something else instead of going for Sacred like you usually would.

By grabbing [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] here, and destroying [_Fire King Avatar Barong_] with Sacred's effect, we are able to play slightly into [_Nibiru, the Primal Being_] territory while ensuring we have a backup plan if Nibiru does hit the field.

Coupled with [_Duelittle Chimera_] and [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_], we generate a lot of card advantage while still setting up a few layers of interruption - let's see it in action.

#### Steps
##### Your turn
- Normal Summon [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_].
- Activate Ulcanix to destroy [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] in your hand and add [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Ponix to Special Summon itself from hand.
- Activate Ponix to add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sanctuary; place [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
- Activate Island to destroy Ulcanix and add [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] from Deck to hand.
- A chain forms:
    - Activate Sacred in GY to Special Summon itself.
    - Activate Ulcanix to Special Summon a [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] from Deck.
- Activate Sacred to destroy [_Fire King Avatar Barong_] from Deck.
- Link Summon [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_].
- Activate Princess to Special Summon something from the GY.
- Link Summon [_Duelittle Chimera_].
- Pass turn.

##### Opponent's turn
- In the Standby Phase, activate Barong's effect to add any Fire King you need from Deck to hand.
- You can start the interruption chain either by using Princess' effect in the GY, to destroy Chimera and Special Summon herself, or by using Kirin's effect in hand to destroy Chimera.
- Remember to use Chimera's effect and add something back from the GY to your hand.

> If you get hit by Nibiru, you can just pass, wait for Barong's effect to resolve, and use Kirin to destroy the searched monster and Special Summon itself. This gives you a FIRE on the field to destroy with Princess and get the interruption chain started.
