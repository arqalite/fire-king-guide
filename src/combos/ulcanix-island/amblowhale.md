### Amblowhale setup

This route trades Nibiru safety for full combo - you get access to [_Fire King Sanctuary_] and also get to do a Sunlight Wolf recycle while ending on Amblowhale backed up by a Kirin in hand.

#### Steps
- Activate [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
- Activate Island; destroy [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_] to search [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_].
- A Chain forms:
    - Activate Sacred; Special Summon it.
    - Activate Ulcanix; Special Summon [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_].
- Activate Sacred; destroy [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_].
- Activate Kirin; Special Summon Ulcanix from GY.
- Activate Ulcanix; destroy Sacred for Ponix.
- Activate Ponix; Special Summon it.
- Activate Ponix; add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sanctuary with no effect.
- Link Summon [_Salamangreat Sunlight Wolf_] using High Garunix and Ulcanix.
- Link Summon [_Relinquished Anima_] using Ponix.
- Activate Wolf; add Kirin from GY to hand.
- Link Summon [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_].
- Activate Princess; Special Summon anything from GY.
- Link Summon [_Amphibious Swarmship Amblowhale_].
