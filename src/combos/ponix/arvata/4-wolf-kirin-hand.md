## Sunlight Wolf setup (with Ponix + High Kirin in your hand)

This line plays similarly to the [Ponix + any FIRE] line, using a [_Garunix Eternity, Hyang of the Fire Kings_] to extend during your turn. 

However, instead of ending on a Level 8 [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_], you can leverage Arvata's effect and make a [_Salamangreat Sunlight Wolf_], under which you can summon [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] to recover a card from the GY.

### Steps
1. Normal Summon [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_].
2. Activate Ponix: add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from your Deck to your hand.
3. Activate Sanctuary: place [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
4. Activate Island: destroy Ponix, then add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from your Deck to your hand.
5. Activate Sacred: Special Summon it from your hand.
6. Activate Sacred again: destroy [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_] in your Deck.
7. Activate Ulcanix: Special Summon [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] from your Deck.
8. Xyz Summon [_Garunix Eternity, Hyang of the Fire Kings_].
9. Activate Hyang, detaching Sacred; target Island to destroy it.
    - At resolution, use Sanctuary's effect to destroy Hyang instead.
10. Activate Hyang again; Special Summon Ulcanix from the GY.
11. Activate Ulcanix: destroy [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_], add [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] from your Deck to your hand, <span style="color:darkorange;"><b>then declare on resolution that Ulcanix is now Level 8.</b></span>
12. Activate Arvata; Special Summon High Garunix from GY.
13. Link Summon [_Salamangreat Sunlight Wolf_].
14. Pass turn.

#### Opponent's turn
13. In the Standby Phase, activate Ponix: add it from the GY to your hand.

> All the caveats, benefits and tricks you can do with the [Ponix + any FIRE] line apply here as well - so make sure you read that page!

<details>
    <summary><b>If Nibiru hits</b></summary>
    <br/>
    Regardless of where your opponent chooses to activate Nibiru, you will only lose access to Sunlight Wolf - you can still play just fine with the Ponix + High Kirin in your hand, but you can no longer recover a card from the GY.
</details>
