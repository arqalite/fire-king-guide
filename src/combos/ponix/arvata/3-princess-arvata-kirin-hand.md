## Promethean Princess + Arvata setup (with High Kirin in your hand, and a 3rd monster on the field)

Variating further on the previous line, you can instead opt to go for [_Salamangreat Sunlight Wolf_] and add High Kirin to your hand instead of summoning it to the field.

It's a very open-ended line, giving you access to a few different plays you can make before passing turn to the opponent.

### Steps
1. Normal Summon [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_].
2. Activate Ponix: add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from your Deck to your hand.
3. Activate Sanctuary: place [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
4. Link Summon [_Salamangreat Almiraj_].
5. Activate Island: destroy [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_], then add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from your Deck to your hand.
6. A chain forms:
    - CL1: Activate Sacred: Special Summon it from your hand.
    - CL2: Activate Arvata: Special Summon Ponix from the GY.
7. Activate Sacred again: destroy [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] in your Deck.
8. Activate High Kirin: Special Summon Arvata from the GY.
9. Link Summon [_Salamangreat Sunlight Wolf_] using Almiraj and Sacred.
10. Link Summon [_Relinquished Anima_] using Ponix.
11. Activate Sunlight Wolf: add High Kirin from the GY to your hand.
12. Link Summon [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_] using Sunlight Wolf and Anima.

> You can further extend this board by using Promethean Princess' effect, giving you multiple options:
>
> - Special Summon Sunlight Wolf under the other Extra Monster Zone, forcing opponents on Link decks to let you recover another card from the GY (or try to play around it).
> - Special Summon Ponix, which can be used by Arvata, High Kirin or Promethean Princess' effects, or can just be destroyed by Hyang so it returns to your hand next turn.
> - Special Summon Almiraj, in some niche applications where you need to protect Arvata/Promethean Princess from being destroyed by your opponent.
> - Summon any FIRE monster, then Link Summon [_Hiita the Fire Charmer, Ablaze_] using Promethean Princess and the summoned monster, thus setting up Promethean Princess in the GY while still keeping Arvata online.
