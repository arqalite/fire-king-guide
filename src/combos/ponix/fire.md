# Ponix + any FIRE monster

You can push the limits of what the Fire King engine is "supposed" to do, by Xyz Summoning [_Garunix Eternity, Hyang of the Fire Kings_] during your turn, then use its effect in combination with [_Fire King Sanctuary_] to make Hyang destroy itself, to further set up and extend.

This leads to an unassuming board ending on a Level 8 [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_], with [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] and [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] in hand - however it lets you make a 4-material Hyang during the opponent's turn, if you so choose!

### Steps
1. Normal Summon [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_].
2. Activate Ponix: add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from your Deck to your hand.
3. Activate Sanctuary: place [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
4. Activate Island: destroy Ponix, then add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from your Deck to your hand.
5. Activate Sacred: Special Summon it from your hand.
6. Activate Sacred again: destroy [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_] in your Deck.
7. Activate Ulcanix: Special Summon [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] from your Deck.
8. Xyz Summon [_Garunix Eternity, Hyang of the Fire Kings_].
9. Activate Hyang, detaching Sacred; target Island to destroy it.
    - At resolution, use Sanctuary's effect to destroy Hyang instead.
10. Activate Hyang again; Special Summon Ulcanix from the GY.
11. Activate Ulcanix: destroy the FIRE monster in your hand, add [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] from your Deck to your hand, <span style="color:darkorange;"><b>then declare on resolution that Ulcanix is now Level 8.</b></span>
12. Pass turn.

#### Opponent's turn
13. In the Standby Phase, activate Ponix: add it from the GY to your hand.

> This line does have caveats - wasting a Hyang can potentially limit your grind game potential in [_Pot of Extravagance_] lists - like the one this guide is based on - and the FIRE monster used for combo is not recovered in any meaningful way.
>
> That said, the ability to set up a 3-4 material Hyang (depending on whether you want to leave High Kirin on the field to be destroyed) allows for plenty of power and flexibility.
>
> Also, by having Kirin and Ponix in hand, you are less vulnerable to board breakers like [_Raigeki_], [_Lava Golem_], etc, and you can choose when to start interrupting your opponent.



<details>
    <summary><b>If Nibiru hits</b></summary>
    <br/>
    Regardless of where your opponent chooses to activate Nibiru, you will only lose Ulcanix, allowing you to still play just fine, but it reduces your follow-up on turn 3.
</details>

<details>
<summary><span style="color:darkorange;"><b>Destroying Island</b></span></summary>
<div class="warning">
It is entirely plausible to NOT protect Island at step 9, and let Hyang actually destroy it. This would lead to exactly the same endboard due to Island destroying Hyang, letting you continue as usual. 

The downside is that it would substantially hurt your grind game, as you no longer have access to Island's effects.

The upside, however, is that it would remove a liability from the board - the opponent might try to remove Island through non-destruction effects, causing your board to blow up - so in certain matchups this could be useful!
</div>
</details>
