# Ponix + High Garunix

This opening hand offers a pretty powerful line that ends on a Hyang setup, with [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_] **and** [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] in your GY, allowing for a very flexible interruption sequence during the opponent's turn.

That said, be aware that this line _does_ play into [_Nibiru, the Primal Being_]. We are able to recover and end on [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] with Promethean Princess in GY, however it's a much weaker endboard.

> If you're certain your opponent runs Nibiru in their deck, it might be wiser to perform the [Ponix + any FIRE Beast, Beast-Warrior or Winged Beast] line instead.

### Steps
1. Normal Summon [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_].
2. Activate Ponix: add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from your Deck to your hand.
3. Activate Sanctuary: place [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
4. Link Summon [_Salamangreat Almiraj_].
5. Activate Island: destroy [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_],  then add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from your Deck to your hand.
6. Activate Sacred: Special Summon it from your hand.
7. Activate Sacred again: destroy [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] in your Deck.
8. Activate Arvata: Special Summon Ponix from the GY.
9. Link Summon [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_].
10. Activate Promethean Princess: Special Summon Arvata from the GY.
11. Pass turn.

#### Opponent's turn
13. In the Standby Phase, activate High Garunix: Special Summon it from the GY,
14. Activate High Garunix again: destroy all other monsters on the field.
15. <b><span style="color:darkorange;">Do not activate Sacred's effect here!</span></b> Instead, activate Arvata: Special Summon Sacred from the GY.

<details>
  <summary><b>If Nibiru hits</b></summary>
  <br/>

  Nibiru will remove Promethean Princess and Arvata before High Garunix can destroy them, meaning you are unable to get to the Hyang setup.
  
  Instead, your first interruption will now be Promethean Princess destroying High Garunix, and then in the next chain, you must also activate Sacred's effect to revive itself. 
  
  Sacred must then destroy [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_], which will Special Summon High Garunix, and destroy a card.

  This basically forces you to use two interruptions at once, leaving you with [_Garunix Eternity, Hyang of the Fire Kings_] as your last.
</details>
