# Sanctuary combos

Opening [_Fire King Sanctuary_] directly gives you some advantages as you no longer need to Normal Summon neither Ponix nor Ulcanix.

First, you play under [_Nibiru, the Primal Being_] more easily as your summon count is reduced by 1-2 monsters. However, this also means you might lack enough bodies to reach some endboards, so sometimes you might open Sanctuary + Ponix/Ulcanix and still choose to Normal Summon Ponix first.

Second, you can use your Normal Summon to strengthen your endboard. For example, Normal Summoning [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] in order to grab a [_Fire King Sky Burn_], or to bait your opponent's handtraps can be a good idea before committing anything else to the board.
