# Ponix + Ash Blossom

[_Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring_] quite often plays double duty in our deck because she's a very powerful interruption that's also a FIRE monster, thus giving you the option to use her as fodder for [_Fire King Island_] if needed.

Using her in this way poses the following question - is it worth deviating from the [Ponix + any FIRE lines] in order to recover her from the GY? In a lot of situations, the answer is "yes".

We have two main ways to recycle Ash Blossom - either immediately, through the [Sunlight Wolf recycling pattern], or on a delay, through [_Duelittle Chimera_]'s on-destruction effect.
