### Amblowhale setup (with Duelittle Chimera in GY)

This route chooses to generate a little more value by ending on a Amblowhale, alongside a Kirin in hand, recycled via Sunlight Wolf. [_Duelittle Chimera_] is in the GY, ready to be revived off Amblowhale, then later destroyed to recover another card from the GY.

#### Steps
- Normal Summon [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_].
- Create the following Chain:
    - CL1 Ulcanix effect to destroy a FIRE and search.
    - CL2 Kirin to destroy a FIRE and Special Summon itself.
- Resolve the chain:
    - Kirin destroys Ulcanix and enters the field.
    - Ulcanix destroys Kirin and searches [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_].
- A new chain forms:
    - CL1 Ulcanix to Special Summon [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] from Deck.
    - CL2 Kirin to Special Summon Ulcanix from GY.
    - CL3 Ponix to Special Summon itself.
- Activate Ponix to add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sanctuary; place [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
- Activate Island to destroy Ulcanix and add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sacred to Special Summon itself from hand.
- Activate Sacred to destroy [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] from Deck.
- Activate Arvata to Special Summon Ulcanix from GY.
- Link Summon [_Salamangreat Sunlight Wolf_] using Sacred and High Garunix.
- Link Summon [_Duelittle Chimera_] using Ponix and Ulcanix.
- Activate Sunlight Wolf to add Kirin from GY to hand.
- Link Summon [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_] using Chimera and Wolf.
- Activate Princess to Special Summon anything from GY.
- Link Summon [_Amphibious Swarmship Amblowhale_].
