### Amblowhale setup (with Ponix & Kirin in hand, Duelittle Chimera in GY)

This route chooses to end on an [_Amphibious Swarmship Amblowhale_], with both [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] and [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] in hand, via the Sunlight Wolf recycling pattern. Additionally, we set up [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_] and [_Duelittle Chimera_] in the GY to take full advantage of Amblowhale's effects.

#### Steps
- Normal Summon [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_].
- Form the following Chain:
    - CL1 Ulcanix to destroy and search;
    - CL2 Kirin to destroy and Special Summon;
- Chain resolves:
    - Kirin destroys Ulcanix and Special Summons itself.
    - Ulcanix destroys Kirin and adds [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] from Deck to hand.
- Another Chain forms:
    - CL1 Kirin to revive;
    - CL2 Ulcanix to Special Summon;
    - CL3 Ponix to Special Summon;
- Chain resolves:
    - Ponix Special Summons itself;
    - Ulcanix Special Summons [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] from Deck.
    - Kirin Special Summons Ulcanix from GY.
- Activate Ponix; add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sanctuary; place [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
- Activate Island; destroy Ponix and add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sacred; Special Summon it.
- Activate Sacred; destroy [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] from Deck.
- Activate Arvata; Special Summon Kirin from GY.
- Link Summon [_Salamangreat Sunlight Wolf_] using 2 monsters.
- Link Summon [_Duelittle Chimera_] using the other 2 monsters.
- Activate Wolf; add Kirin from GY to hand.
- Link Summon [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_].
- Activate Princess; Special Summon something from GY.
- Link Summon [_Amphibious Swarmship Amblowhale_].

##### Opponent's turn
- In Standby Phase, activate Ponix to add itself back to hand.
