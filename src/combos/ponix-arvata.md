# Ponix + Arvata

Thanks to its on-destruction effect, opening [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] gives you access to a lot of flashy, high-ceiling combos and endboards. It also gives you the most flexibility as you end up generating a lot of Link Material that you can use in any way you want. It's arguably our most powerful starting hand, provided you don't get interrupted at all during setup. 

That said, interruption is plentiful in today's game, and as long as [_Nibiru, the Primal Being_] floats around, a lot of the flashy plays involve a lot of risk. Instead, you can use Arvata to your advantage to shield yourself from Nibiru, while still making a powerful board.

Here is where we introduce the Amblowhale + Arvata endboard - as its name suggests, you can end on an [_Amphibious Swarmship Amblowhale_] backed up by Arvata, while having performed a [Sunlight Wolf recycle] to grab [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] from the GY. All this while being protected by Nibiru, as Arvata hits the field at the 5th summon exactly, and stays there throughout the combo.

Of course, you can also play lower to the ground if you want, and still reap some benefits - ending on Princess + Arvata now nets you a [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_] in the GY which can be revived for utility or a Rangbali negate as needed.
