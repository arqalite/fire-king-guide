# Ulcanix + High Avatar Garunix

As you might already know, opening [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] means most Fire King decks won't be able to use Ulcanix's on-destruction effect - that limits your choices when deciding what to destroy with [_Fire King Island_] and [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_].

That said, this starting hand doesn't lend well to any kind of Link plays, as we don't have any way to stop Nibiru. We also need to pay attention to High Avatar Garunix's effect and avoid letting him destroy our board during the Standby Phase, if we choose to go this route. Usually reviving him with Arvata/Kirin does the job.

...or we can plan for it, and let him blow up our cards to generate a bit more advantage. Let's take a look!

#### Steps
##### Your turn
- Normal Summon [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_].
- Activate Ulcanix to destroy [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] in your hand and add [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Ponix to Special Summon itself from hand.
- Activate Ponix to add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sanctuary; place [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
- Activate Island to destroy Ponix and add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sacred to Special Summon itself.
- Activate Sacred to destroy [_Fire King Avatar Barong_] from Deck.
- Link Summon [_Duelittle Chimera_] using Sacred and Ulcanix.
- Pass turn.

##### Opponent's turn
- In the Standby Phase, in separate chains:
    - Activate Ponix's effect to add itself back to hand.
    - Activate Barong's effect to add [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] from Deck to hand.
    - Activate High Avatar Garunix's effect to Special Summon itself.
    - Activate High Avatar Garunix's effect to destroy all other monsters (including Duelittle).
    - Activate Duelittle Chimera's effect to add back Barong from GY to hand.

##### Interruption
When you're ready to interrupt: 
- Activate Kirin to destroy Barong in hand and Special Summon itself.
- Activate Sacred's effect to Special Summon itself from GY.
- Activate Sacred's effect to destroy a Fire King from Deck to suit your needs. 

> Example follow-up:
> - Destroy [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] with Sacred. 
>   - We mostly want Arvata in GY to revive it later, so don't activate its effect unless you have a good target - _leave Barong in the GY!_.
> - You can make Hyang early, using Sacred and High Garunix, to blow up Kirin and your opponent's cards. 
> - Kirin can then revive Ulcanix. Use her effect to pop Hyang and search Rangbali. 
> - Hyang will revive 2 cards (Arvata + High Garunix), and Rangbali will enter the field, giving you a monster negate, a Spell/Trap negate, and High Garunix's board wipe if you destroy him for either negate. 
> - You still have a Barong ready to search in the Standby Phase too, and a Ponix in hand for turn 3 plays.
