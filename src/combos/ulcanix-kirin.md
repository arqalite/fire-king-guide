# Ulcanix + High Avatar Kirin

This starting hand is very explosive, being able to generate a lot of Link material for us to play with. Coupled with the generic Ulcanix combos, there are many lines you can choose from.

Additionally, by having [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] already in the GY, we can instead opt to send [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] off [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_], setting it up in the GY either to be revived as part of our endboard, or later during the opponent's turn,

That said, we really want to keep Kirin in our hand, so more often than not you'll want to go through Sunlight Wolf and get it back after you combo with it.
