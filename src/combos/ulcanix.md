# 2-card Ulcanix combos

As discussed in her own page, [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_] plays many roles in our deck. As a starter, she builds upon the Ponix lines, giving you a way to search [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] and enable its Special Summoning condition. Later on, she becomes an extender after being destroyed, allowing you to generate enough material for Link or Xyz plays.

However, she comes with the caveat that you will **always** require a second FIRE monster in your hand besides her. While Ponix can set up [_Garunix Eternity, Hyang of the Fire Kings_] by itself, Ulcanix does nothing without having another FIRE to destroy. As such, _there is no 1-card Ulcanix combo_, unfortunately.

> It's also worth noting that most Ulcanix lines will have 1 more Summon than the equivalent Ponix line - this pushes most combos into [_Nibiru, the Primal Being_] territory, so it's something you need to watch out.

That said, she brings a lot of power and flexibility to the table, making higher-ceiling endboards easier to reach. Let's dive in!
