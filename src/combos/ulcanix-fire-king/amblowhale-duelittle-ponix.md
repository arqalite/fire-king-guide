### Amblowhale setup (with Ponix in hand, Duelittle in GY)

This route combos a little further, ending on an [_Amphibious Swarmship Amblowhale_]. In the GY we set up a [_Duelittle Chimera_] that can be revived by Amblowhale, providing a nice ATK boost. When destroyed, it recovers a card from the GY, and also triggers Amblowhale's second effect, destroying one of your opponent's card.

By destroying [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] with [_Fire King Island_], it returns to our hand in the Standby Phase, ensuring we have a turn 3 play.

#### Steps
- Normal Summon [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_].
- Activate Ulcanix; destroy the Fire King in your hand and add [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Ponix; Special Summon itself from hand.
- Activate Ponix; add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sanctuary; place [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
- Activate Island; destroy Ponix and add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Sacred; Special Summon itself.
- Activate Sacred; destroy [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] from Deck.
- Activate Arvata; Special Summon a Fire King from GY, **except Ponix**.
- Link Summon [_Duelittle Chimera_].
- Link Summon [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_].
- Activate Princess; Special Summon a FIRE monster from GY, **except Ponix**.
- Link Summon [_Amphibious Swarmship Amblowhale_].

##### Opponent's turn
- In Standby Phase, activate Ponix to add itself back to hand.
