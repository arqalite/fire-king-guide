### Hyang setup (with Kirin in hand)

Depending on matchup and current metagame, it can be worth playing minimally and avoiding unnecessary risks. A Kirin in hand can power up a simple Hyang setup and give you a few more interruptions to use against your opponent.

#### Steps
- Normal Summon [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_].
- Activate Ponix's effect to search [_Fire King Sanctuary_].
- Activate Sanctuary from hand, place [_Fire King Island_] in Field Zone.
- Activate Island's effect to destroy Ponix and search [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_].
- Activate Sacred Garunix's first effect to Special Summon itself from hand.
- Activate Sacred Garunix's second effect to destroy [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_] from deck.
- Activate Ulcanix's effect to Special Summon [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] from Deck.

#### Interrupting your opponent
This might seem like just a 1-card Ponix combo, as Kirin sits in our hand unused during our turn, but watch what happens when we use it at the right time during the opponent's turn, after we Xyz Summon [_Garunix Eternity, Hyang of the Fire Kings_].

- Activate [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] in hand, destroying [_Garunix Eternity, Hyang of the Fire Kings_] to Special Summon itself.
- Activate Hyang - Special Summon [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] and [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_] from GY.
- A chain forms:
    - Activate Sacred - destroy [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] from Deck.
    - Activate Ulcanix - destroy Kirin and add [_Fire King Avatar Rangbali_] from Deck.
- Another chain forms:
    - Activate Kirin - Special Summoning Arvata (and destroying a card if needed).
    - Activate Arvata, targeting [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] to Special Summon it.
    - Activate Rangbali in hand to Special Summon itself.

You now have a Spell/Trap negate, a monster negate, and a High Avatar Garunix waiting to be destroyed in the End Phase, wiping the board again in the Standby Phase. That triggers some of your Fire Kings, starting the cycle once again and giving you resources for a turn 3 follow-up.
