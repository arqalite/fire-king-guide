# Ulcanix + any FIRE monster

At its core, [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_]'s role as a starter is to give you access to [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_], as long as you have another FIRE monster to destroy. 

As such, the basic combo ends on a similar result as the [1-card Ponix] combo: 2 Level 8 monsters, and [_Fire King Sanctuary_] ready to Xyz Summon during the opponent's turn.

#### Steps
1. Normal Summon [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_].
2. Activate Ulcanix - destroy the FIRE monster in your hand, then add [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] from your Deck to your hand.
3. Activate Ponix - Special Summon it from your hand.
4. Activate Ponix again - add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from your Deck to your hand.
5. Activate Sanctuary - place [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
6. Activate Island - destroy Ponix, then add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from your Deck to your hand.
7. Activate Sacred - Special Summon it from your hand.
8. Activate Sacred again - destroy Ulcanix on your field.
9. Activate Ulcanix - Special Summon [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] from your Deck.
10. Pass turn.

##### Opponent's turn
11. In the Standby Phase, activate Ponix - add it from the GY to your hand.
