### Promethean Princess + Arvata setup (without Sanctuary)

As mentioned above, this route calls back to the [Ulcanix + any FIRE Beast, Beast-Warrior or Winged Beast] line.

You can use Ulcanix's effect to chainblock [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_]'s effect that Special Summons it, ensuring Sacred cannot be hit by a [_Dominus Impulse_] or any kind of interruption that would end your turn right there.

As you already have enough material this way, you don't need to use [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] anymore.

> That said, if Ulcanix's effect somehow gets negated, you do need to use Arvata, choosing to Special Summon the 2nd Ulcanix from the GY.

#### Steps
1. Normal Summon [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_].
2. Activate Ulcanix - destroy the 2nd Ulcanix in your hand, then add [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] from your Deck to your hand.
3. A chain forms:
    - CL1: Activate Sacred - Special Summon it from your hand.
    - CL2: Activate the Ulcanix in GY - Special Summon [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] from your Deck.
4. Activate Sacred again - destroy [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] in your Deck.
5. Link Summon [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_].
6. Activate Promethean Princess - Special Summon Arvata from the GY.
