# Ponix + High Avatar Kirin

[_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_], as noted in its individual discussion, is an amazing card that does a lot for our strategy. It often extends our combos when destroyed from the Deck through [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_]'s effect, for example.

As a starting piece, it offers a lot of flexibility at the cost of playing into [_Nibiru, the Primal Being_]. Depending on matchup and the current meta, Kirin hands might benefit from playing minimally and avoiding unnecessary risks. That said, when Nibiru isn't a concern, this hand can end on very strong endboards.
