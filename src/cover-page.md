<center><h1>The Fire King Sanctuary</h1>

<div>
Hello and welcome to the fire king guide. 

Whether or not you are here as a new yugioh player considering picking up the Fire King Structure Deck to start your Yu-Gi-Oh! journey or a seasoned player looking to get yourself familiar with the archetype, this is the right place for you. This guide is aimed to help guide players who just picked up Fire King to get started.

The guide will mostly consist of combo guides, what handtraps to watch out for and how to play around them, and also niche rulings and interaction between Fire King cards. This guide is continuously being worked on, so expect future updates to come along.

<b> Do be reminded that just remembering the combo is not all there is to fire king. You are not playing a solitaire combo deck, you are playing a midrange deck. How you decide on a combo is dictated by what you have available in your hand and what your opponent does. Getting better comes with practice and not just memorizing combos.</b>

<b> The most important to do, however, is to just have fun when playing the deck! Click on the exploding ponix to get started</b>
