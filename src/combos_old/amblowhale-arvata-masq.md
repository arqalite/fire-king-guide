# 2-card Amblowhale + Arvata setups (with I:P Masquerena in GY)

When playing uninterrupted, we have multiple options when it comes to our own layers of interruption.

One choice you will frequently encounter is whether to set up [_I:P Masquerena_] in the GY (to be revived by Amblowhale), or whether to add a [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] back to hand using [_Salamangreat Sunlight Wolf_]. Masquerena is a powerful interruption, especially when paired with [_S:P Little Knight_], but Kirin offers you much more utility and resiliency against your opponent's plays.

In this page, we'll cover the lines that go for setting up [_I:P Masquerena_] in the GY.

> As before, these can be risky to perform against strategies that run a lot of non-engine or have interaction on the opponent's turn.
>
> Only the routes starting with Arvata in hand can play through [_Nibiru, the Primal Being_], and you're expected to give your opponent about 4 draws into [_Mulcharmy Fuwalos_].
>
> Use your best judgement to determine if it's worth extending up to this point.
