# 2-card Amblowhale + Arvata setups (with High Kirin in hand)

Having a [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] in your hand can offer a safety net against your opponent's attempts at breaking your board, allow you to extend further during your own turn, or just general utility.

Thus, it can often be a good idea to choose to perform a [Sunlight Wolf recycle] instead of setting up [_I:P Masquerena_] in the GY.

> As before, these can be risky to perform against strategies that run a lot of non-engine or have interaction on the opponent's turn.
>
> Only the routes starting with Arvata in hand can play through [_Nibiru, the Primal Being_], and you're expected to give your opponent about 4 draws into [_Mulcharmy Fuwalos_].
>
> Use your best judgement to determine if it's worth extending up to this point.
