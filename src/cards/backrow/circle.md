Circle is a fun card, with two main uses.

The first one is being an OTK tool, see the [OTK combos]. You
can destroy [_Garunix Eternity, Hyang of the Fire Kings_] to revive a FIRE monster from the GY, and then using Hyang's effect you can revive even more monsters to quickly fill up your side of the field.

Additionally, Circle can be used to dodge any kind of targeting disruption on your FIRE monsters. This is most useful on [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] and [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_], helping you use their effects through an [_Effect Veiler_] or [_Infinite Impermanence_] for example.

**We recommend playing 1 copy**, but you can skip if if you don't like it.

> Remember you can always search this card with [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_].
