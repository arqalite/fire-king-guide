This is a lore card that Konami probably intended you to play in either Fire Kings or Atlanteans, but it sucks in both?

Anyway, the FIRE effect of this card is just bad - you have to wait a turn in order to destroy a card, then discard a card. Assuming you destroy an opponent's monster, you just went -1 (used the trap and discarded a card, to remove your opponent's monster). If you destroy one of your own cards you could potentially go plus, but when [_Fire King Sky Burn_] exists, why bother?

**We don't recommend running any copies of this card.**
