# The Spells & Traps

The Fire King archetype has 6 backrow cards: 5 Spells, and one Trap card.

There are also two lore-related cards, 1 Spell and 1 Trap, and we'll talk about them too (spoiler alert: they're not particularly good).

Of notable highlight are [_Fire King Island_] and [_Fire King Sanctuary_], both of which are essential parts of the Fire King engine. They help you get cards into rotation, and in the case of Sanctuary, it ensures follow-up during the opponent's turn.

Let's get started by talking about them first!
