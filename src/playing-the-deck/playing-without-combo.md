# Playing without combo

So far, we looked at Fire King through the lens of a combo deck, that wants to open 1 or more of these starters:
- [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] (with or without another FIRE monster)
- [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_] + a FIRE monster
- [_Fire King Sanctuary_] / [_Fire King Island_] + an assortment of monsters

However, we haven't really looked at what to do when your opening hand doesn't have any starters. At first glance, you might think those hands are unplayable and that's why we didn't talk about them, but that's not necessarily true.

Every combo or mid-range Yu-Gi-Oh! deck will give you opening hands of various quality across hundreds or thousands of shuffles. If you were to collect statistics on it, that may look something like this:

- A vast majority of hands had at least 1 starter
- A significant amount of hands did not have a starter, but were playable
- A lower number of hands were total bricks and you were not able to execute your game plan at all

Depending on the deck, those playable hands can still result in something, and that's very much the case for our deck.

Fire King has the advantage of being very reactive, as we have a plethora of monsters that do something when they, or another card is destroyed. Thus, even when we do not have combo, we can still put up a fight by using these effects wisely.

Let's take a deeper look and see what we mean by that exactly.
