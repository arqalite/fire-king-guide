# Combos

In this section we'll discuss the combos you'll perform most of the time, featuring our two starters - [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] and [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_].

Instead of focusing on the starting hands themselves, we'll focus on the resulting endboards, discuss their pros and cons, and the multiple routes you can take to achieve them.

First off, we'll talk about basic setups for [_Garunix Eternity, Hyang of the Fire Kings_], including the 1-card Ponix combo, the combo that all the other ones build upon.

Then we'll look at a mid-range endboard consisting of [_Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames_] and [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_], which gives you a lot more power without being too vulnerable to your opponent's non-engine. This section will also show how to play under [_Mulcharmy Fuwalos_] and show a few lines that can stop [_Nibiru, the Primal Being_].

Following that, we'll talk about the complete endboards this deck can produce, namely those centering around [_Amphibious Swarmship Amblowhale_]. These combos are more fragile (and play into both Fuwalos and Nibiru) but come with a lot more power and utility, and can win you the game if successfully completed.

Finally, we'll close out this chapter with a few OTK combos, useful for going second, or during the mid-to-late game where you're confident you can end the game.

Let's dive in!
