This absolutely adorable critter makes things awkward for us.

Under Meowls, we have to make the following changes to our combos in order to limit the amount of draws you're giving your opponent:
- Try to destroy [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_] off [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_] - this gives you a [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_] from Deck, avoiding the GY entirely.
- If you have to destroy [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_], try to Special Summon something from hand with its effect, instead of the GY.

This makes most combos give only 1-2 draws, and some 3-card hands can give 0.

For example, Ulcanix + Arvata (or Rangbali) can play under Meowls using the two tweaks from above, provided you have another FIRE monster in your hand. Let's take a look:

- Normal Summon [_Fire King Courtier Ulcanix_].
- Activate Ulcanix's effect to destroy the FIRE in your hand and add [_Legendary Fire King Ponix_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate Ponix's effect to Special Summon from hand.
- Activate Ponix's effect to add [_Fire King Sanctuary_] from Deck to hand.
- Activate [_Fire King Sanctuary_]; place [_Fire King Island_] in the Field Zone.
- Activate Island, destroying Ulcanix for [_Sacred Fire King Garunix_].
- A chain forms:
  - Ulcanix's effect to Special Summon [_Fire King High Avatar Garunix_].
  - Sacred's effect to Special Summon itself.
- Activate Sacred's effect to destroy [_Fire King High Avatar Kirin_] from Deck.
- Activate Kirin's effect to Special Summon [_Fire King Avatar Arvata_] **from hand**.
- Do [Sunlight Wolf recycling] with the 3 monsters except Arvata if you want.
- Link Summon Princess and end your turn on Princess + Arvata.

> Sadly, if you start with Ponix, you're either forced to give your opponent 1 draw by playing normally, or do the 1-card [Hyang setup].
